The problem

Most enterprises own the tools. The configuration doesn’t match reality.

If your network discovery, physical facility mapping, and voice configuration aren’t in sync, you’re carrying real risk. It shows up in most of the enterprise UC environments I’ve assessed.

Network Discovery

"What’s actually on your network?"

Physical Facilities

"Do your floors and rooms match your data?"

Voice Configuration

"Does your CUCM E911 logic reflect reality?"

When these three systems aren’t aligned, a 911 call from the wrong device delivers the wrong location to a dispatcher. That’s a life-safety failure and a legal liability.

The process

Three phases. Measurable outcomes.

From a free initial review through ongoing assurance, each phase builds on the last.

01

Complimentary

15-minute strategic review

A working session to map the requirements of Kari’s Law and RAY BAUM’s Act against your current configuration and identify immediate liability gaps. No obligation beyond the conversation.
