The Story Behind Solution Track

I am Stephen Frahm, the founder of Solution Track Consulting. My approach to IT is shaped by two decades of high-stakes engineering and a background defined by service and exploration.

As a veteran, I built my professional foundation on discipline and the "mission-first" mindset. In the world of enterprise communication, where a down system or a compliance gap can have real-world consequences, that discipline is what ensures my clients stay operational and protected.

Beyond the data center, I am an avid world traveler. Navigating diverse cultures and complex international landscapes has sharpened my ability to communicate across technical and business silos. I bring that same adaptability to every forensic audit, helping teams bridge the gap between legacy technical debt and modern cloud strategy.

Why Solution Track Consulting?

With over 20 years of experience in the Cisco UC ecosystem, I’ve seen that technical failures are rarely the fault of the hardware. They are the result of poor discovery. I started this firm to provide a forensic alternative to the "staff-aug" status quo, giving PNW firms and global partners a senior-level perspective they can trust.
