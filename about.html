About Stephen Frahm

I started STC because senior-level UC engineering was too hard to find.

I have spent 25 years working inside complex Cisco UC environments. CUCM, Contact Center, UCCX, Unity, Webex, Amazon Connect. Large enterprises, multi-site deployments, migrations that should have been straightforward and weren’t. I have seen what happens when the wrong person is in the room on a high-stakes engagement. I started Solution Track Consulting so there would be a better option.

Every engagement at STC is led and delivered by me. Not a team you interviewed once and never saw again. If you have a UC environment with real problems and real stakes, that is the work I am here for.

Over 25 years, I have built a network of experienced engineers I can draw on across the full IT landscape, not just UC. When a project needs additional depth, I bring in people I have worked with and trust. You still deal with me. The work still gets done right. Most of my work comes through referral or repeat engagement. That is by design.
